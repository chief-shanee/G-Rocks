## 3. HAZARD IDENTIFICATION
- **Primary Hazards**: Not for ingestion. Vaporized form may cause altered states, elevated heart rate.
- **Signal Word**: WARNING
- **Risk Statements**:
  - May affect heart rhythm (due to Yohimbine and PEA).
  - Avoid high-temperature combustion (max: 150°C).
  - Consult physician before use.

## 4. FIRST AID MEASURES
- **Inhalation**: Move subject to fresh air. If symptoms persist, seek medical attention.
- **Eye Contact**: Rinse with clean water for 15 minutes.
- **Skin Contact**: Wash with soap and water.
- **Ingestion**: DO NOT induce vomiting. Seek emergency medical attention.

## 5. FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES
- **Suitable Extinguishing Media**: Dry chemical, foam, CO₂.
- **Special Equipment**: Fire-resistant respirators if product is aerosolized.

## 6. HANDLING & STORAGE
- **Storage Conditions**: Cool, dry, dark environment. Store in sealed packaging.
- **Handling Advice**: Use gloves and avoid inhalation of raw powder.
- **Container Materials**: Amber glass or sealed mylar with desiccant.

## 7. EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION
- **Eye Protection**: ANSI-certified goggles recommended.
- **Skin Protection**: Gloves when handling raw concentrate.
- **Respiratory**: Use fume hood or filtered mask if vaporizing in enclosed space.

## 8. PHYSICAL AND CHEMICAL PROPERTIES
- **Appearance**: Large translucent crystalline shards
- **Color**: White / Opalescent
- **Melting Point**: 130-150°C
- **Odor**: Minty-sweet with bitter trace
- **Solubility**: Soluble in PG, partially in ethanol
- **Density**: ~1.2 g/cm³

## 9. STABILITY AND REACTIVITY
- **Stability**: Stable under recommended storage conditions
- **Incompatible Materials**: Strong acids, oxidizing agents
- **Hazardous Decomposition**: None under normal use

## 10. TOXICOLOGICAL INFORMATION
- **Acute Toxicity**: Low at recommended dose (50mg)
- **Carcinogenicity**: No evidence
- **Neurotoxicity**: No effect at standard vaporization levels (up to 100mg)
