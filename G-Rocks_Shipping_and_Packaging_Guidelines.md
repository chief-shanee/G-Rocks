# G Rocks Shipping and Packaging Guidelines

---

### **Introduction**
To ensure compliance with legal and regulatory standards, and to maintain the integrity of G Rocks, clear and comprehensive packaging and shipping guidelines must be followed. This document outlines the key aspects of product packaging for both bulk and gram-based sales, including weight, labeling, storage instructions, and safety warnings.

These guidelines are crucial for protecting the product during transit, ensuring that it is stored and shipped properly, and providing necessary information to customers about its safe use.

### **1. Packaging for Bulk Sales**
When selling G Rocks in bulk, proper packaging is essential to prevent contamination and ensure the product’s potency. Each bulk shipment should be securely sealed and labeled as follows:

- **Packaging Material:** Use food-safe, tamper-evident containers made from durable materials (e.g., high-quality Mylar bags, glass jars, or plastic containers). These materials must be non-reactive and able to withstand exposure to heat or moisture during transit.
- **Weight:** Clearly mark the weight of the contents on the packaging. Bulk sales should be sold in increments of at least 100g. The packaging must indicate the exact weight to ensure customer transparency.
  - Example: “Net Weight: 100g” or “Net Weight: 500g”
- **Sealing:** Ensure all packages are tightly sealed to prevent exposure to air and moisture. Use heat-sealing or airtight options to ensure product integrity.
- **Storage Instructions:** Include clear instructions on how to store the product to preserve its quality:
  - “Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and humidity.”
  - “Keep tightly sealed to avoid contamination.”
- **Safety Warnings:** Ensure the following warning is prominently displayed:
  - “Keep out of reach of children.”
  - “Use only as directed.”
  - “Consult a healthcare provider before use, especially if you have a medical condition.”
- **Labeling:** The label must contain the following information:
  - Product name: **G Rocks**
  - List of ingredients
  - Legal disclaimers
  - Net weight
  - Storage instructions
  - Safety warnings

### **2. Packaging for Gram-Based Sales**
For individual gram-based sales, packaging must be compact, secure, and clearly labeled. The packaging should protect the product from contamination and damage.

- **Packaging Material:** Use high-quality, child-resistant, resealable pouches (Mylar bags with zip-lock seals or equivalent) to ensure product protection and safety. Packaging must be opaque to shield the product from light exposure.
- **Weight and Dosage:** Clearly mark the exact weight of the contents on each package. For single-serve sales, the packaging should clearly display the weight and ensure portions are measured accurately. 
  - Example: “Net Weight: 1g” or “Net Weight: 5g”
  - If a product is being sold with a specific number of servings, ensure that the number of servings per package is clearly indicated (e.g., "Contains 20 servings of 50mg").
- **Sealing:** Ensure that each package is securely sealed to prevent contamination or exposure. The resealable feature is recommended for consumer convenience.
- **Storage Instructions:** Include instructions for proper storage to maintain product quality:
  - “Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight.”
  - “Seal package tightly after use to preserve freshness.”
- **Safety Warnings:** Include the following warnings in large, readable text:
  - “Keep out of reach of children.”
  - “Use only as directed.”
  - “Consult a healthcare provider before use, especially if you have a medical condition.”
  - “Not for use by persons under 18 years of age.”
- **Labeling:** The label for gram-based sales should include:
  - Product name: **G Rocks**
  - List of ingredients (with proper scientific names and concentrations)
  - Legal disclaimers
  - Net weight or volume (e.g., “Net Weight: 1g”)
  - Storage instructions
  - Safety warnings
  - Recommended dosage
  - A clear "Manufactured by" or "Distributed by" statement (include the company name and contact details)
  - QR Code (if applicable) directing to the product’s website for more information (optional but recommended for traceability)

### **3. Shipping Guidelines**
Shipping G Rocks in bulk or in grams must meet the following requirements:

- **Shipping Containers:** Use sturdy shipping containers, such as cardboard boxes, to hold the product securely during transit. The containers should be adequately cushioned to prevent damage. 
- **Shipping Labels:** Ensure that shipping labels are clear, accurate, and securely affixed to the package. These should include:
  - Shipping address
  - Return address
  - Weight of the package
  - Any applicable tracking number
- **Compliance with Shipping Regulations:** All shipments should comply with local, state, and international shipping laws. Ensure that the product is shipped in accordance with the regulations of the region it is being sent to.
- **Tracking and Documentation:** Ensure each order shipped has a tracking number. Keep records of all shipments and customer orders to ensure traceability in case of issues or returns.

### **4. Legal and Regulatory Compliance**
G Rocks contains ingredients that are legally restricted in certain regions (e.g., Kratom is illegal in some states or countries). It is important to comply with the legal regulations regarding the sale and distribution of such products.

- **State-Specific Legal Considerations:** Ensure that you are fully informed about the legality of selling ingredients such as Kratom, Yohimbine, and PEA in specific states or countries. Only ship to locations where it is legally permitted.
- **International Shipping Considerations:** If shipping internationally, verify the legal status of each ingredient in the destination country. Some countries may have strict import regulations regarding psychoactive substances, and it is important to ensure that the product can be legally imported.

### **5. Customer Safety and Disclaimer**
By purchasing G Rocks, customers acknowledge that they are responsible for understanding the legal status of the product in their jurisdiction. It is recommended that customers check the local laws and regulations before purchasing or consuming G Rocks.

**Important:** Include a disclaimer with each package shipped, such as:

> "By purchasing and consuming G Rocks, you acknowledge that you are aware of the legal and health risks associated with its use. It is your responsibility to ensure that this product is legal in your region and to use it only as directed."
