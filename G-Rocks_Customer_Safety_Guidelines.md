# G Rocks Customer Safety & Usage Guidelines

---

### **Recommended Dosage**
- **Single Serving:** 50mg (1 puff) - This is the standard dosage for G Rocks.
- **Frequency:** Limit usage to no more than 3 times per day. For regular use, take breaks of at least 4 hours between doses to avoid overuse.
- **Maximum Daily Intake:** 150mg (3 puffs) is the upper safe limit. Exceeding this dosage may increase the risk of adverse effects.

**Important:** Begin with the minimum recommended dosage of 50mg (1 puff). If you're new to this product or similar substances, start with half the recommended dose to assess tolerance.

### **Potential Side Effects**
While G Rocks is designed to enhance cognitive function, mood, and energy, it contains potent psychoactive ingredients that may cause side effects in some individuals, including but not limited to:

- **Kratom Alkaloids (Mitragynine/7-OH-Mitragynine):**
  - Potential **nausea**, **dizziness**, or **drowsiness** at higher doses.
  - **Euphoria** may be intense for some users, potentially leading to **dependency** with chronic use.
  - Prolonged use may cause **withdrawal symptoms** when discontinued suddenly.

- **Yohimbine HCl:**
  - Increased **heart rate**, **anxiety**, **irritability**, **insomnia**, and **elevated blood pressure**.
  - Overuse can lead to **nervousness** or **restlessness**.

- **Phenylethylamine (PEA):**
  - May cause **headaches**, **tachycardia** (increased heart rate), **elevated blood pressure**, and **nausea** in some users, especially in sensitive individuals.
  
- **Rhodiola Rosea, L-Theanine, and Huperzine A:**
  - Rare side effects include **drowsiness**, **headaches**, or **gastrointestinal discomfort**. 

### **Precautions**
- **Consult with a Physician:** We highly recommend that individuals consult with a healthcare provider before using G Rocks, especially if they have:
  - **Heart conditions** (e.g., hypertension, arrhythmias).
  - **Mental health issues** (e.g., anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia).
  - A history of **substance abuse**, as this product may lead to psychological dependence.
  - **Pregnant or breastfeeding women** should avoid using this product due to the potential risks to the fetus or infant.
  
- **Use in Moderation:** G Rocks contains several stimulant ingredients, and overuse may cause negative health consequences. Do not exceed the recommended dosage. For individuals with underlying medical conditions, including high blood pressure or mental health disorders, **avoid** using this product unless cleared by a physician.

- **Avoid Alcohol and Other Stimulants:** Do not combine G Rocks with other stimulant substances, including but not limited to caffeine, energy drinks, or alcohol, as this may increase the risk of side effects like heart palpitations, anxiety, or dizziness.

### **Addiction and Dependency Risks**
- **Psychological Dependence:** Due to the stimulant and euphoric effects of ingredients like Kratom and Yohimbine, there is a risk of developing psychological dependence with excessive or prolonged use.
- **Withdrawal Symptoms:** Sudden cessation of use after regular consumption can lead to withdrawal symptoms, including irritability, anxiety, and lethargy. If experiencing withdrawal symptoms, **gradually reduce the dosage** or consult a healthcare professional for guidance.

### **Storage and Handling Instructions**
- **Store in a cool, dry place** away from direct sunlight and moisture to preserve the quality and potency of the product.
- **Keep out of reach of children** and pets. G Rocks is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

### **Emergency and Overdose Instructions**
- In the case of suspected overdose or adverse reaction:
  - **Stop using immediately.**
  - Seek medical attention if you experience severe symptoms like **rapid heart rate**, **difficulty breathing**, **chest pain**, or **extreme agitation**.
  - **Call Poison Control** or go to the nearest emergency room if you believe you or someone else has ingested an excessive amount of the product.

### **Final Notes**
G Rocks is a powerful supplement containing psychoactive ingredients, and it should be used responsibly. Always follow the recommended dosage, and be mindful of how it may interact with other substances or conditions. This product is intended for adult use only. For the safety and well-being of all users, we strongly encourage moderation and responsible consumption.
