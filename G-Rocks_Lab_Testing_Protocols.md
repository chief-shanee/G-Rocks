### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**  
This dossier outlines the **G-Rocks Laboratory Testing Protocols**, establishing a **multi-tier quality verification system** in alignment with **U.S. FDA, EU REACH, German DIN, and Chinese SFDA** regulatory standards. **Failure to adhere to these protocols will result in immediate non-compliance classification and restricted trade operations.**  


### **I. COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORKS & MANDATORY REGULATORY STANDARDS**  
- **FDA Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) Compliance** – 21 CFR Part 58  
- **EU ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation** – Analytical Testing & Calibration  
- **German DIN EN 45502-1:2020** – Pharmaceutical & Chemical Safety  
- **China SFDA Analytical Chemistry Standards** – Class I & II Compounds  


### **II. SAMPLE COLLECTION & INTEGRITY MAINTENANCE**  
- **Chain of Custody Documentation**: All collected samples must be tracked under **high-security data protocols** with electronic verification logs.  
- **Tamper-Proof Containers**: Samples must be sealed in **GMP-compliant, tamper-evident vials**, serialized and barcoded for traceability.  
- **Environmental Controls**: **Storage at -20°C ±2°C** for stability. **All test samples expire after 120 days.**  


### **III. ANALYTICAL TESTING PROCEDURES & METHODOLOGIES**  
#### **1. Alkaloid Purity & Potency Testing**  
- **Methodology**: High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC)  
- **Detection Limit**: 0.01% w/w (mg/mg)  
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Mitragynine ≥ 98% purity  
  - Yohimbine HCl ≥ 99% USP standard  
  - Huperzine A (1%) – Conforms to nootropic-grade benchmarks  

#### **2. Heavy Metals & Contaminant Screening**  
- **Methodology**: Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS)  
- **Maximum Allowable Limits (MALs)**:
  - Lead (Pb): < 0.5 ppm
  - Arsenic (As): < 0.2 ppm
  - Mercury (Hg): < 0.1 ppm  

#### **3. Microbiological & Pathogen Testing**  
- **Methodology**: Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) Screening  
- **Screened Pathogens**:
  - Salmonella spp.
  - Escherichia coli (E. coli)
  - Staphylococcus aureus  
- **Result Requirements**: **Negative for all contaminants.**  

#### **4. Solvent Residue & Volatile Organic Compound (VOC) Analysis**  
- **Methodology**: Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS)  
- **Acceptable Residue Levels**:
  - Ethanol < 5000 ppm  
  - Propylene Glycol < 1000 ppm  
  - Methanol: Not detected

- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for Data Access**: Restricted to Level-3 clearance personnel only.
