## IV. GMP COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

**G Rocks™, Inc.** hereby affirms that its manufacturing facility adheres to the following standards:

### 🔧 A. Quality Assurance Systems

- Written **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** for:
  - Ingredient testing
  - Sanitation
  - Batch records
  - Worker hygiene
  - Equipment calibration
- Each production lot is assigned a **Lot Number** for traceability.
- Finished product testing includes:
  - Microbial screening (E. coli, Salmonella, Yeast, Mold)
  - Heavy metals analysis (Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium, Mercury)
  - Water activity and shelf stability evaluation

### 📦 B. Ingredient Handling Protocol

- All ingredients are:
  - **100% traceable** to origin (COAs retained)
  - Stored under optimal humidity and temperature
  - Quarantined upon arrival until full QC release

### 🔍 C. Documentation & Records

- GMP logs retained for **minimum 3 years**
- Master Manufacturing Records (MMRs) & Batch Production Records (BPRs) maintained digitally with access control
- Labeling approval logs preserved for traceability

### 🛡️ D. Staff Training

- All personnel are trained in:
  - GMP handling procedures
  - Adverse event reporting
  - Contamination prevention
